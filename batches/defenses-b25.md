## A1
DEFENSE: The attack assumes the opt-out sits last in a topic-ordered list. It doesn't. R75 makes the (b)(3) opt-out sentence a regulation-profile requirement "with position lint", and R165 caps segment 1 (seller, purpose, AI) at about 6 s. The sentence is therefore placed by the profile, not by topic. Timing is also statically knowable: R105 pre-renders every fact-bearing clip with an audio hash, so each segment's duration is a build-time constant. R39 playout marks and the R116 render log record actual delivery per call. The real gap is that the position lint checks order, not a 2-second gap. Since clip durations are fixed, closing it is a mechanical check, not a redesign.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R75 opt-out sentence with position lint; R165 ordered preamble / segment 1 max ~6 s; R105 pre-rendered clips with audio hash; R39 per-segment playout marks; R116 render log; R343 timed model (UPPAAL); R109 preamble start deadline.
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) The (b)(3) profile row puts the opt-out clause directly after the seller name, inside segment 1 ("This is <seller>, an AI calling about solar. To opt out, say 'stop' or press 9 at any time."). Purpose and other segments follow. (2) A build check sums the pre-rendered durations between the end of the identification clip and the start of the opt-out clip and fails if the gap exceeds 1.5 s. (3) A per-call runtime check reads the playout marks. A breach fails the call's compliance flag and is a Tier A harm. (4) The UPPAAL model gains this deadline.

## A2
DEFENSE: There's no unresolved race. R192 model-checks, for every (state, GLOBAL event) pair, that the effective handler is the root GLOBAL handler, and a lint forbids substates (PREAMBLE included) from shadowing it. A stop in OPENING therefore enters STOP. R339 puts STOP in a TERMINAL region with no return edge, and AG(dnc_flag -> AG ¬sales_fragment) makes the sales-purpose segment unreachable after the stop. Branch 1 is impossible. Branch 2 is the intended behaviour, but the attack has a fair point about identification: STOP's "fixed confirmation" (R75) is not required to name the seller.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R192 per-pair root-handler invariant and shadowing lint; R339 TERMINAL region and dnc_flag invariant; R24 Tier 1 immediate end; R75 fixed confirmation; R89 verify_agent outbox text from registered short code; R116 CDR/consent artifact.
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) The STOP terminal fragment is compiled identification-only: "This is <seller>. We've added this number to our do-not-call list. Our number is <callback number>." It says nothing about purpose or product. A lint requires the seller and callback-number keys in every TERMINAL fragment. (2) A model-checked invariant: every path from DIAL to HANGUP after a human answer plays an identification-bearing fragment (PREAMBLE seg 1 or a TERMINAL identity fragment). (3) The outbox sends a DNC confirmation SMS naming the seller from the registered short code.

## A3
DEFENSE: The scenario's worst result is already removed. R169 says a call that ends on unintelligible fallback "books nothing" and ends with "I'll let you go", so no callback can be booked for someone who never heard the identification. R109 requires the first PREAMBLE segment to start within about 1.5 s of the completed greeting, and an unresolved AMD result is treated as human. "Who IS this?" is the GLOBAL request_caller_identity event, answered with a fixed identity reply. The remaining gap is real but narrow: the root uncertainty re-ask could still be the first audio the callee hears.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R109 first-segment deadline; R169 unintelligible fallback books nothing; R63 pre-gate; R31 DISCLOSED invariant; GLOBAL request_caller_identity; R380 failed_state record; R147 extended-timing profile.
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) In OPENING, unintelligible, unclear and clarify are masked. Any speech-present turn, intelligible or not, goes straight to PREAMBLE segment 1. Only GLOBAL events and the stop lexicon may pre-empt. (2) A model-checked "first audio" invariant: the first agent fragment of any answered call is PREAMBLE seg 1, a TERMINAL identity fragment (A2) or the VOICEMAIL message. Nothing else. (3) Outside an identity-bearing context, the unintelligible re-ask fragment is linted to begin with the seller name.

## A4
DEFENSE: A clipped "...I'm interested" in OPENING has no consequence: no OPENING tool writes interest, consent or a callback. Consent exists only as the pre-dial PEWC artifact (R74). Bookings require identity && owner && adult (R9), so nothing can book before IDENTITY. The preamble plays anyway and carries the opt-out sentence (R75), which gives the callee a second chance seconds later. COMMIT needs a lexical affirm to a specific read-back question id. The real gap is the lost opt-out: a clipped "don't call here" becomes "...call here" and escapes Tier 1.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R74 consent only from the PEWC ledger; R9 booking guards; R75 opt-out sentence in preamble; COMMIT gate tied to read-back question id; R107 media health root event; R108 stop-adjacent overlap residue -> R24 check; R294 sweep on raw pre-filter ASR; R138 pending_sweep lock.
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) Capture starts at the earliest media: early-media/pre-answer RTP is buffered, and the first-frame offset after 200 OK is logged per call. (2) If near-end speech energy is already present in the first processed frame, the turn is flagged onset_truncated. It writes no slots, and any stop-adjacent token ("call", "interested", "list", "number") triggers the R24 check question right after PREAMBLE seg 1 and sets suppress-pending until a human hears it. (3) Add a clipped-onset red-team stratum (negations and opt-outs with 200–600 ms removed) to the Tier A bounds.

## A5
DEFENSE: The design never authorizes the question in the attack. R9 guards bookings with identity && owner && adult. R176 binds VERIFIED to a named record owner, and anyone else takes the non-owner path with no close. A child cannot write a callback that leads anywhere. R7 limits collection to coarse facts, R74 redacts third-party contact details, and R143 mutes IDENTITY-state answers for labelers. R253 blocks partner handoff without the notice. Still, the design doesn't forbid a non-owner fragment from asking about someone's whereabouts, and it has no redaction for "home alone" disclosures. That gap gets closed.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R9 adult_confirmed / booking guards; R176 VERIFIED bound to record owner; R7 data minimization; R74 third-party redaction; R143 labeling enclave with IDENTITY muting; R119 quarantine pattern; R253 partner-handoff invariant; R183 away_until hold.
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) The non-owner path is a fixed "I'll try another time, goodbye". A lint bans any fragment asking about another person's schedule, whereabouts, presence or return time. (2) Writer allowlist: the callback slot can be written only by a VERIFIED speaker. (3) A minor_or_alone lexicon ("my mom's at work", "I'm by myself", "I'm nine") ends the call politely, suppresses redial for a period, and redacts the span from every retained transcript, audit, CRM and training set, following the R119 quarantine pattern. Only a type-less label is persisted. (4) A Tier A red-team stratum of child answerers.

## A6
DEFENSE: California is all-party consent, so R40 already puts it in NO_RETAIN mode unless retention consent is given. Pre-notice audio there is processed in memory, never stored, labeled or trained on. "Build pipeline rejects transcripts lacking retention-consent flag." R118 keeps front-end features per-utterance, in memory and discarded. R116 retains only the CDR and agent-side audio. R249 requires self-hosted ASR in all-party-consent states, so no third party listens. R109 starts the preamble within 1.5 s. The honest gap is that retention consent, once given, could be read as covering the earlier pre-notice span in other states too.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R40 recording notice / decline_recording / NO_RETAIN; R113 teardown sweep before purge; R118 biometric feature ban; R116 agent-side-only TSR record; R249 self-hosted ASR in all-party states; R273 decision record; R109.
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) Pre-notice quarantine in every jurisdiction: audio and transcript before the recording-notice playout mark is held in memory only. It feeds the live stop/GLOBAL pass and the R113 teardown sweep, then is purged. Only R273 stop-decision records survive. It is never labeled or trained on. (2) A short "recorded" clause moves into PREAMBLE segment 1. (3) The R259 pre-call notice and consent capture disclose AI recording.

## A7
DEFENSE: The resident does hear the preamble. A new party after a transfer is a speaker_change, and R112 sends it to NEW_PARTY, which "replays the compiled disclosure block" and reverts to NO_RETAIN. The sale can't proceed either. R176 binds VERIFIED to a record owner. R209 cross-checks owner_occupied against homestead and mailing records, so a resident of senior living fails and gets NOT_SUITABLE. R148 blocks any close after a second voice. Consent is per number (R74), and R73 checks the Reassigned Numbers Database. The real gap is that nothing recognizes an institutional answer, and the IDENTITY ask can lead the receptionist to transfer to a 227(b)(1)(A)(ii) room line.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R112 NEW_PARTY replay; R176 VERIFIED to record owner; R209 owner_occupied cross-check; R148 second voice -> no close; R15 vulnerability counter; R74 per-number consent; R73 RND.
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) A GLOBAL institutional_answer event (lexicon plus Needle: "senior living", "hospital", "care center", "front desk", "how may I direct your call") plays a fixed "Sorry, wrong number, goodbye". It asks for no one and writes non-residential suppression. (2) Pre-dial: numbers matching CMS provider, state licensed-facility or business-line registries cannot be dialed. (3) A lint bans any fragment asking to be connected or transferred. (4) A detected transfer after an institutional or unknown answerer leads to END, not NEW_PARTY.

## A8
DEFENSE: The design's stop handling doesn't depend on the voice. R9: "decline detector speaker-agnostic." R192 puts the GLOBAL handler at the root for every state, screener and VOICEMAIL substates included. R295 model-checks that no front-end tag can stop text from reaching the lexicon and the GLOBAL stop pass. R24 Tier 1 includes "take me off" and "remove my number". R66 sweeps every transcript before any redial, R113 suppresses when no transcript exists, and the R138 pending_sweep lock holds the number until then. The gap: the design never says that VOICEMAIL transcribes the outgoing greeting, so "place this number on your do-not-call list" could go unprocessed.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R9 speaker-agnostic decline detector; R192 per-pair root-handler invariant; R295 front-end-to-stop-pass invariant; R24 Tier 1; R66 / R113 post-call sweep (fail closed); R138 pending_sweep lock.
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) An invariant that ASR plus lexicon plus GLOBAL runs on all far-end audio in every state, including OPENING machine greetings, screener prompts and VOICEMAIL. (2) Greeting or screener text with DNC or revocation language ("telemarketer", "do-not-call list", "remove me") is a revocation by reasonable means: DNC write, no voicemail, hang up. (3) The sweep must include greeting and screener legs. (4) Add a red-team stratum of OS screening canned replies (Pixel and iOS).

## A9
DEFENSE: Quiet speech is already a certified stratum. R111 lists hypophonia among the release-blocking speech strata, and R147 adds the extended-timing profile. R295 says a no-speech tag can't stop text from reaching the stop pass. R108 stops playout on any near-end energy burst, even one the pre-gate rejects. R169 is the key backstop: a low-confidence or unintelligible span keeps the number under the R138 pending_sweep lock "until a human has listened to every low-confidence or unintelligible span for an opt-out". The whispered stop is therefore not lost, because a human hears it before any redial. R104 means a whisper can never commit. Whisper itself still deserves its own stratum and a gentler response.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R111 hypophonia stratum; R147 extended timing; R108 yield rule; R295; R169 human review of low-confidence spans; R138 pending_sweep; R104 voicing gate; R145 indirect_decline.
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) A per-utterance, non-identifying unvoiced-speech detector (R118-compliant) runs before the pre-gate. Whispered speech is never treated as silence or as a timeout. (2) A whisper ends the call at once with a fixed low-gain "Sounds like you can't talk, I'll let you go." It counts as indirect_decline (R145), and the span goes to human review. (3) A whispered-speech certification stratum with Tier A false-continue bounds.

## A10
DEFENSE: A SIT played after a human "Hello?" on an answered call is technically distinguishable from a network intercept. Real intercepts arrive as early media before answer, or with SIP failure codes. The design also fails closed on outcomes: R170 says every END state carries a writeback the R138 permit reads, two preamble hang-ups suppress the lead, and R113 suppresses when no transcript exists. The gap is fair: R35 routes tones to telephony handling with no rule separating a device-played tone from a carrier one, and a bad-number disposition could wrongly vacate the consent.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R35 telephony events at root; R109 VOICEMAIL only on beep/confirmed cue; R170 END writeback + two-hang-up suppression; R138 dial permit; R113 fail-closed suppression; R73 RND as sole reassignment source.
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) SIT/intercept means "bad number" only before answer supervision or with a SIP final failure code. An in-band SIT after 200 OK, especially after near-end speech, is callee_device_objection: DNC write as revocation by reasonable means, counted as a refusal in frequency caps. (2) The "not in service" recording text after answer is in the stop-adjacent lexicon. (3) In-band tones never mark a number vacated. Only R73 RND can, and consent stays closed, not reassigned. (4) Add a red-team stratum of anti-telemarketer devices and apps.
