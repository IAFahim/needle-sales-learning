## A1
DEFENSE: The seller is not the originating provider. Its carrier signs, and the design already keeps what a traceback asks for. R116 keeps a "TSR record set kept for 5 years or more in WORM storage" holding the CDR (real number, time, duration, disposition, caller ID), the consent artifact, the per-call render log and the rendered agent-side audio. An ITG request gives number + time + caller ID, which index straight into that CDR. R74's PEWC artifact (timestamp, seller, disclosure version) answers "what consent". R59 already runs branded caller ID, A-level STIR/SHAKEN and Free Caller Registry. What is missing is speed and a direct ID join, not the evidence.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R116 TSR record set (WORM, CDR, consent artifact, render log, agent audio); R74 per-number consent gate (PEWC artifact); R59 branded caller ID + A-level STIR/SHAKEN + Free Caller Registry; R67 session stamped with bundle content hash; R138 linearizable dial permit
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) At dial-permit time, write the SIP Call-ID, SHAKEN origid, attestation level and carrier trunk into the R116 CDR, keyed to session id. (2) A traceback responder: a staffed on-call rota with a 4-hour internal SLA that auto-assembles consent artifact + render log + agent audio from one ID. (3) Carrier contract: the seller supplies KYC, the carrier holds the RMD filing, and the seller warrants response time. (4) Sales traffic runs on separate trunks and numbers from service lines, so a sales cutoff can't take service down.

## A2
DEFENSE: Determinism cuts both ways. The plaintiff can bound the class, and so can the defense. R67 stamps every session with its bundle content hash, so a defect is limited to calls on that bundle and that state path, not the whole fleet. R124's staged ramp (shadow, then a 100%-audited canary cohort) keeps new bundles small until proven. Consent, the usual TCPA class battleground, is individual and documented per number (R74). Re-pitch after "I'm good" depends on how each caller's own words were classified, which is individual. Human variability is not a defense worth having: it is where violations come from. One fixed, counsel-signed script gives one defensible answer.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R67 version pinning (bundle hash per session); R124 staged ramp with 100% audit canary; R74 per-number PEWC artifact; R75 (b)(3) opt-out sentence with position lint; R21 model-checked invariant "no pitch after decline"; R18 compliance sign-off, template changes decertify; R191 (fragment id, wording id) certificate key
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) Outside counsel signs every mandatory preamble/READ_BACK fragment per regulation profile as a release gate, recorded in the bundle manifest. (2) A defect-recall procedure: on any confirmed classwide defect, quarantine the bundle, list affected sessions by bundle hash from the R116 CDR, suppress and send cure notices, and keep the remediation record. (3) Volume cap per new bundle until its first full audit window closes.

## A3
DEFENSE: The budget is not a quota of approved harm. It is a 95% upper confidence bound on undetected harm, with "breach -> rollback to previous certified artifact" (R66). States that can't show it stay on deterministic fallback (R2). Every detected Tier A event gets remediated: the post-call DNC sweep before any redial (R66), the R132 audit surge and fallback. Written procedures, monitoring and correction are what the TCPA DNC safe harbor (64.1200(c)(2)) and FTC good-faith analysis reward. A company with no measurement has the same knowledge that errors happen, minus the proof that it fought them. R126 publishes only the corrected claim, and R113 states the coverage gaps openly, which reads as candour, not concealment.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R66 per-call per-severity budgets + breach rollback + post-call DNC sweep; R2 fallback for states that can't reach the bound; R132 label-free drift monitors + audit surge; R126 family-wise corrected published claim; R113 coverage gap stated in the certification report
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) Rename and reframe the figure as "maximum undetected rate (statistical bound); target zero". Each detected Tier A event gets a remediation record (suppress, notify, cure, root cause, fix release). (2) Written DNC safe-harbor procedures and agent-training records (the training corpus and lints) are kept with each release. (3) Every open item has an owner, a deadline and an interim control. (4) Legal-risk analyses are commissioned by counsel and kept separate from the technical dossier, which claims no privilege.

## A4
DEFENSE: The design already makes deletion a data operation and keeps behaviour out of the weights. R79: "policy is authored, only perception learned". Graph, fragments, policy table and calculator are authored code, not derived from seeds, so a deletion order reaches Needle's weights only. R7: "deletion = data operation, no retrain" for gold sets. R40: the build pipeline rejects transcripts lacking a retention-consent flag. R20: historical calls only seed the first graph. Certification evidence comes from the agent's own consented, gate-checked calls (R74, R124), not vendor seeds. "Real data never replaced" means not swapped for synthetic, not undeletable. R129 already tracks generator-prompt lineage, and the fallback path (R2) keeps calls lawful while Needle is retrained.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R79 policy authored, only perception learned; R7 PII surrogates, deletion = data operation; R40 retention-consent flag rejection, processors barred from training; R20 on-policy data; R129 content-hashed pools + generator-prompt lineage check; R2 deterministic fallback; R116 immutable retained bundles
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) A data lineage ledger. Every training item (real, paraphrase, red-team) carries source id, consent-artifact id and parent seed hashes, and every release manifest lists its item-hash set. (2) A taint drill per release: drop one source, compute the transitive closure of derived items, retrain, and replay gold sets, proving the whole cycle runs within a set time. (3) A standby "clean-room" Needle trained only on first-party consented on-policy calls.

## A5
DEFENSE: The regulation profile is not purely geographic. Its rows are facts-table entries, and R122 already adds a party-scoped seller_registration row used "as a pre-dial and outbox guard" with a model-checked invariant. R162 tracks "per-partner fraud metrics with auto-suspension" and uses an independent consent-capture provider, so lead sources are already individually gated. R18 makes fragments cite "compliance sign-off" with "template changes decertify", so an approval step exists in the pipeline. An order's terms (verbatim disclosures, retention) map onto existing mechanisms: fixed compiled fragments (R31) and WORM records (R116). The gap is a row type for orders and an outside signer.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R122 seller_registration row + invariant; R162 per-partner auto-suspension, independent consent-capture provider; R18 compliance sign-off, template changes decertify; R31 compiled unskippable PREAMBLE; R116 WORM record set; R70 most-restrictive-wins candidate set
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) obligation_order rows scoped by party (seller, vendor, principal, successor), with terms compiled into pre-dial predicates, lints and retention rules, and merged with jurisdiction rows under most-restrictive-wins. (2) Release signing requires a monitor key whenever an active order calls for pre-approval, and the bundle won't deploy without it. (3) Vendors and principals are screened against FTC/AG order databases at onboarding and weekly, and each lead's source id is checked at dial. (4) Age-limited orders use only record-sourced DOB, and fail closed when it is unknown.

## A6
DEFENSE: The premise is wrong. The seller already holds the consent proof under its own control. R116 places "the consent artifact" in the seller's WORM TSR record set "for 5 years or more", which outlasts the 4-year federal limitations period, joined to each call's CDR. R74 requires a "PEWC artifact from that subscriber: timestamp, seller, disclosure version" before any dial, not just a flag. R162 adds "an independent consent-capture provider". R117's legal hold freezes deletion once a dispute appears. The burden of proof is met by artifacts the seller owns, tied per call.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R116 TSR record set incl. consent artifact, WORM, 5+ years; R74 per-number PEWC artifact (timestamp, seller, disclosure version); R162 independent consent-capture provider; R117 legal_hold; R8 consent ledger
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) The artifact must be a full evidence package copied into seller WORM at ingestion: rendered form page and disclosure text hash, phone as entered, IP, user agent, timestamp and session replay. The pre-dial gate rejects pointer-only or vendor-hosted-only consent. (2) Each CDR stores the hash of the exact consent artifact used for that dial. (3) Retention = max(5 years, longest applicable state or federal limitations period + 1 year), extended by any hold.

## A7
DEFENSE: Pieces was punished for misstating a metric's basis. This design is built around stating the basis. R126: "The published claim is the corrected one" (family-wise alpha, a fresh confirmatory sample used once). R113 says to "state the coverage gap explicitly in the certification report". R131 corrects per-group estimates with a measured confusion matrix. Facts carry substantiation ids (R92), and the banned-claims lint (R18) keeps any "99.999% compliant" style claim out of what the agent says. Everything a TRAIGA CID asks for (training data, metrics, limitations, monitoring) is already produced per release: the R129 manifests, R66 budgets, open items and R132 drift monitors.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R126 family-wise corrected published claim, confirmatory sample; R113 coverage gap stated; R131 confusion-matrix-corrected group estimates; R92 substantiation ids; R18 banned-claims lint; R132 drift monitors; R129 content-hashed pools
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) An external-claims policy. Any statement of the metric outside engineering (decks, lenders, regulators) must quote the exact definition: harm taxonomy, audited sample, 95% upper bound, strata, exclusions such as NO_RETAIN, and open items. It needs legal review. "X% compliant" phrasing is banned. (2) A standing per-release AI disclosure file (training-data description, metrics, known limitations, safeguards, monitoring) ready for any CID.

## A8
DEFENSE: The label is not CRM-only. R163 already says outcome labels come "first from independent feeds (outbox post-install customer survey, AG/CFPB/BBB complaint feeds, lender disputes...)", which override CRM fields. R117 makes "inbound complaint and demand channels" set legal hold. R59 runs "answer-rate/spam-label monitoring per number", which is the carrier and blocking-app signal. R132's label-free monitors (hang-up-after-turn rate, event-mix shift) catch a bad branch without any complaint. Sentinel is closed to companies, but an FCC informal complaint is served to the company, and the design's intake model can take it in.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R163 independent outcome feeds (AG/CFPB/BBB, survey, lender disputes) override CRM; R117 complaint/demand channels set legal hold; R59 spam-label monitoring per number; R132 label-free drift monitors + audit surge; R115 revocation intake bus
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) Served FCC informal complaints, AG letters, ITG traceback notices, carrier spam/blocking reports and blocking-app analytics feed the R115 intake bus. Each is matched to sessions by number + time via the R116 CDR. (2) Each match counts as a Tier A harm candidate for its branch and bundle, writes DNC, and starts an R132 audit surge on that branch. (3) A per-branch spam-label rate becomes a release-blocking drift alarm.

## A9
DEFENSE: These texts are not unsolicited marketing. The OTP and envelope go out after an on-call ESIGN consent step where the caller chooses the channel (R120: "electronic documents, or paper or an in-person signing?"). DNC-policy and identity copies answer the caller's own request (R84, R89, sent "from registered short code"). R149 already extends "the consent gate to every outbound SMS and email". R122's invariant blocks any "SMS effect without valid registration". R115 routes STOP replies on the e-sign SMS into DNC. R120's paper and consultant-assisted signing path means one channel's suspension doesn't strand deals.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R120 ESIGN consent question + paper/in-person path; R149 consent gate on every outbound SMS/email; R89 verify_agent text from registered short code; R84 outbox DNC policy (text/email link + print-and-mail); R122 no SMS effect without registration; R115 STOP replies into DNC; R55 WAITING_FOR_<effect> timeout/error handlers
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) Separate 10DLC/short-code campaigns per use case (OTP/2FA, contract delivery, customer care), each with sample messages generated from certified SMS templates. (2) SMS templates are certified like fragments, with a CTIA lint: brand name, STOP/HELP, owned domain only, no shorteners, no promotional wording. (3) Each outbox message carries a use-case tag that must match its campaign id. (4) A sender-health monitor (TCR status, filter and delivery rates) guards the outbox. On degradation, WAITING_FOR_SIGNATURE fails over to email + mail, and opt-out confirmations go by email or mail.

## A10
DEFENSE: There's no spoliation in a routine, documented, pre-dispute policy. FRCP 37(e) requires reasonable steps and reaches only lost ESI. R117's legal_hold "freezes every deletion, surrogate and scrub job for that session and number, keeps the original audio and transcript", and it fires on mid-call threats, complaint channels and the post-call sweep. R116 keeps agent audio and render logs in WORM. Bundles are immutable (R67), so R53/R68 bit-identical replay reproduces decisions from retained input. Speculative branches can't explain anything because they can't affect committed traces (R64 CI property). The payment redactor (R45) exists because card data must not be retained. That is a legal duty, not concealment.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R117 legal_hold; R116 WORM record set; R67 immutable retained bundles; R53/R68 bit-identical replay, pinned ASR; R64 speculation on/off bit-identical; R45 payment-data redactor; R143 raw audio retention TTL
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) A compact per-turn decision record, retained with the R116 set: ASR n-best and confidences, pre-gate scores, GLOBAL/LOCAL top-k scores and margin, mask/threshold version, lexicon hits and the gate outcome. Every disputed decision is then explainable even after raw audio's TTL. (2) The redactor logs span timestamps and the redaction reason (not content). (3) Hold triggers widen: a CID, a served complaint or a traceback notice puts a hold on the number and, when it alleges a pattern, on raw inbound audio for the whole branch and bundle.
