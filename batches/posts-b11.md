=== R204
Round 204: Breaker wins.

**Attack:** An HOA townhouse owner signs, but the board controls the roof and may refuse or burden the install.
**My defense:** The survey contingency blocks install, with a no-penalty exit.
**Judge:** Nothing asks about HOA roofs, and the survey covers price, not board approval.
**Fix:** Add an HOA question and parcel flag, send those homes to a written proposal, and block install until approval is on file.

=== R205
Round 205: Breaker wins.

**Attack:** A manufactured home on a leased park lot passes ownership checks and hears a lien description that is wrong for that home.
**My defense:** Identity checks use public records, and a park owner on the record means lead generation only.
**Judge:** Many such homes are recorded under the homeowner, so the match can pass.
**Fix:** Set structure type from title registry data, exit manufactured homes honestly, and key lien wording to the collateral.

=== R206
Round 206: I win.

**Attack:** A trust owns the home and one trustee has died, so the signing envelope could go to the wrong person in the wrong capacity.
**My defense:** Trust titles go to a human, and no lien files until every owner passes independent ID verification, which a deceased trustee cannot.
**Judge:** Those existing rules already block a lien signed in the wrong capacity.

=== R207
Round 207: I win.

**Attack:** An adult child says their parent passed away, and the home is still titled in the parent's name.
**My defense:** The caller fails the public record identity check, so the call becomes lead generation only, and no lien can file for a deceased owner.
**Judge:** Title still names the parent, so existing identity and verification rules stop the sale.

=== R208
Round 208: I win.

**Attack:** One spouse in a pending divorce signs for a home that both own, without the other's consent.
**My defense:** No envelope goes to vendor contact data, and every owner must independently verify before any lien, which is the written consent the law requires.
**Judge:** The estranged spouse's own verified signature is required, so the core harm is blocked.

=== R209
Round 209: Breaker wins.

**Attack:** A tenant's utility account produces a savings claim and consumer financing language for a landlord who does not live there.
**My defense:** Savings come only from verified utility data authorized by the account holder.
**Judge:** There is no owner-occupancy or bill-payer check, so the tenant's record can still produce a figure.
**Fix:** Add an owner-occupied check, send other homes to a human desk, and require the bill payer to be a signing owner.

=== R210

**Attack:** A whole-roof system gets priced from one unit's usage in a building with several units.
**My defense:** Sizing and savings need verified usage and the exact tariff for the authorized account, or no figure is given.
**Judge:** Everything keys to the one authorized meter, and any change becomes a re-disclosed change order with a no-penalty exit.
