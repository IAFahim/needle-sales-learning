=== R214
Round 214: I win.

**Attack:** A battery upsell call reaches a borrower who is 75 days late, her husband answers, and the agent ends up discussing the missed payments.
**My defense:** The caller has to state the name and address themselves, so the agent never names the account, and the hardship phrase leads to a scripted exit with no pitch.
**Judge:** An open service ticket already stops all sales calls, and a marketing call that never mentions the debt is not debt collection.

=== R215
Round 215: I win.

**Attack:** A customer in Chapter 7 bankruptcy gets an upgrade call offering a deal for people in good standing, and the campaign keeps going after discharge.
**My defense:** "I'm in bankruptcy" counts as a hardship phrase, which triggers a scripted exit with no pitch, and the design cannot even produce a good standing offer.
**Judge:** Nothing in the design collects a debt, so a marketing call is not an attempt to collect a discharged one.

=== R216
Round 216: Breaker wins.

**Attack:** A senior says "cancel it", hears it was recorded, mails nothing, and the loan, lien filing and installer slot all stay active.
**My defense:** The cancel step sends written confirmation and a right-to-cancel reminder, and nothing irreversible happens inside the window.
**Judge:** Cancellation reaches only the seller, not the lender, lien or installer.
**Fix:** One cancel now fans out to every party, and the customer hears "started" until all confirm.

=== R218

=== R219
