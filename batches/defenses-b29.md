## A1
DEFENSE: A California, Indiana or Illinois number only reaches the dialer through a certified regulation profile. R305 says "any candidate jurisdiction without a certified regulation profile is a hard no-dial", and R265 has outside counsel sign every mandatory preamble fragment per profile. R331's traceability matrix makes every guard cite a profile row, uses union of obligations, and runs a satisfiability check that sends unsatisfiable mixes to lead-gen or a human. An ADAD live-intro duty the graph can't meet is exactly that kind of unsatisfiable mix. The ADAD row is missing, though. This is a profile-authoring gap, not an architectural one: the R333 human channel already exists.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R305 unprofiled = no-dial; R265 counsel sign-off per profile; R331 traceability matrix + satisfiability check; R8 state rules as facts rows; R333 trained-human capacity token; request_human warm transfer.
NEW HARDENING: NEW adad_rule row on every profile layer: {consent_exemption_attested | live_intro_required | prohibited}, defaulting to live_intro_required until counsel attests otherwise. In live_intro_required layers, a certified human operator (R333 token) gives the unrecorded announcement: name, business address, phone number, and "may I play a recorded message?". Only a lexical yes unlocks the compiled PREAMBLE. Model-checked invariant: no fragment audio plays before live_intro_consent in those layers. No operator capacity = no dial.

## A2
DEFENSE: The number isn't hidden. R259 tells the lead the calling number at consent capture, and the branded A-attested caller ID shows it. R84's request_contact_info gives it as a fixed answer at any moment. R75 already treats (b)-paragraph content as profile-mandated preamble segments with a position lint, and R331 forces every 64.1200 subsection to map to a guard. So (b)(2) is one missing segment key in a mechanism built for it, and it needs no new architecture. Fair point, though: an on-demand answer does not meet an affirmative per-call duty.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R75 profile-required preamble segments + position lint; R84 request_contact_info; R259 number told at capture, fixed branded pool; R165 segmented preamble; R116 render log with playout marks; R331 traceability matrix.
NEW HARDENING: NEW callback_number policy key. It must be a staffed line that is not the dialed ANI, accepts DNC requests, and is checked daily by a live test call. It plays as preamble segment 2, right after the 6-second R165 segment 1, so it counts only on uninterrupted playback. Every END fragment (stop, NOT_SUITABLE, WRAP_UP) repeats it. Model-checked invariant: no path from DISCLOSED reaches hang-up without a playout mark on callback_number, except callee hang-up. R116 proves it per call.

## A3
DEFENSE: R92 limits social proof to audited aggregate policy-table facts with a substantiation id. R94 puts testimonials under endorsement-disclosure rules. R199 makes any quantifier ("9 in 10") require a table key, and R326 juxtaposition lint and the counsel-signed path book judge net impression. R222 requires a legal attestation before a row can be cited. A survey sent only to success accounts was never sanctioned. R163 makes the post-install survey a labeling feed, not a marketing sample, so reusing it as a claim is a row-authoring error. Still, nothing checks the sampling frame mechanically.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R92 aggregate social proof + substantiation id; R94 endorsement disclosures; R199 quantifier lint; R326 juxtaposition lint + path book + "net impression misleading" label; R222 legal attestation; R163 independent outcome feeds.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: type substantiation records. A review_aggregate row must declare its sampling frame. A build check rejects any frame filtered on outcome, satisfaction or complaint status: it must be every signed contract from the R163 e-sign webhook list, cancelled and disputed included, with response rate stated. Until a Part 465 attestation exists, satisfaction and review aggregates are banned from voice fragments entirely. Only countable facts such as installs_in_county stay.

## A4
DEFENSE: This was already fixed. R242 requires every e-sign clause to be a policy-table key, and its material list names "assignment plus the FTC Holder Rule notice". A template with an untagged clause, or a template/READ_BACK version mismatch, fails release. R17 puts the lender's financing disclosures in the e-sign packet, and R217 admits a lender program only after profile review. Wider exposure is what the Holder Rule intends. Our defense is that the spoken claims are certified and true: savings come from R230 modeled_production with a shortfall tolerance, and warranties need R161 backing instruments. A recovery cap clause isn't an explicitly linted pattern yet.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R242 material clause keys incl. Holder notice; R17 e-sign packet with lender disclosures; R217 lender program admission; R154 contract = lender amount invariant; R222 banned-claims lint over contract templates; R230 modeled_production; R161 warranty backing.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: the lender loan-document template version is hashed into the bundle. Lender program admission requires the verbatim 16 CFR 433.2 text to be present. The R222 lint is extended to ban recovery-cap patterns ("shall not exceed amounts paid"). At runtime, CONTRACT_SENT is refused unless the lender envelope's document hash matches an admitted version. No milestone funding is accepted otherwise.

## A5
DEFENSE: The overnight soft-pull scenario contradicts the design. R225 says the customer starts the lender's soft-pull decision "through an outbox link to a lender-hosted page, never by voice", and lender_verified slots have writer allowlists. R7 data minimization gives the agent "no account/income/credit". R73 says "credit/prequal never spoken", and R46 gives every caller the same menu. Credit-filtered lead purchases are a lead-sourcing gap, but the controls are already in place: R268 checks each lead's source id at dial and screens vendors, and R246 requires vendor attestations.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R225 customer-initiated soft pull; slot writer allowlists; R7 no credit data; R73 credit never spoken; R90 credit band only from lender prequal provenance; R46 same menu; R268 per-lead source id; R246 vendor attestation.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: each lead source row declares its selection criteria under vendor attestation. Any credit-derived criterion (FICO band, credit segment, trigger list) rejects the source, because the design runs no FCRA prescreen program (firm offer, 615(d) notice, optoutprescreen scrub). Build check: no pre-dial job may call any lender or bureau API. The lender_verified writer accepts only tokens bound to a customer-initiated R225 session.

## A6
DEFENSE: ROOF_FIRST, per R71, means "inspection before any quote", not a sale of a partner's reroof. R322 names the licensee and licence number per line item and splits the contract when another trade's licensee is involved. R318 restricts third-party battery programs to post-PTO outbox enrollment "under the program's own terms and consent". R321 bans recurring services from voice. Partner data flow is gated by R253 (notice playout-marked, no opt-out), R251 GLBA and R203's allowlisted handoff schema with no billing fields, and R45 means billing data never exists anyway. The one real gap is that a lead-gen warm transfer has no spoken second-seller disclosure.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R71 ROOF_FIRST; R322 per-line licensee; R318 third_party_program; R321 negative_option; R253 partner-handoff invariant; R251 GLBA; R203 handoff schema; R45 no payment data; R47 lead-gen mode.
NEW HARDENING: NEW SELLER_CHANGE state. Any effect whose counterparty differs from the preamble seller first plays a compiled segment: partner identity, sales purpose, nature of goods. It then asks a closed consent question, and the partner's own consent, DNC and R122 registration rows must pass. Model-checked invariant: no cross-seller handoff without that playout mark and a lexical affirm.

## A7
DEFENSE: Most of this is already blocked. R272 certifies SMS templates with a lint against promotional wording, so taglines and "sorry to see you go" links can't render. R342 re-reads the revocation ledger at send time and drops every marketing intent in a stop turn, so "compliance confirmations alone pass". R261 gives every message a [not_before, not_after] window and never sends session-bound items late. R260 has priority classes, and R196 deduplicates by idempotency key. What's missing is a 5-minute cap and a one-message limit specific to revocations.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R272 certified SMS templates + CTIA lint; R342 purpose-tagged drain; R261 send windows, no late sends; R260 priority classes; R196 SMS budgets/idempotency; R75 fixed confirmation; R84 DNC policy by email/mail.
NEW HARDENING: NEW revocation_confirmation template: a single sentence confirming the opt-out, linted to brand ID only, with no link and no persuasion tokens. not_after is set to revocation time + 5 min, it goes in the top priority class, and it is dropped (never sent late) if not delivered by then. One per revocation, deduplicated by (number, revocation). The outbox refuses every other SMS to that number afterward. A requested DNC policy goes only by email or mail.

## A9
DEFENSE: Totals already lead in the design's architecture. R85 makes total cost a mandatory READ_BACK item. R154 says "a financed quote's total is the lender contract price" and requires the envelope amount to equal the lender amount. R309 states the contract price "as all-in" including local taxes. R32 allows only non-triggering financing language before a quote, and R231 limits savings to year one. Massachusetts and California also carry R47-style lead-gen rows in many territories. Fair point, though: the ordering of the first spoken price and the mandatory fees left in the envelope aren't constrained by any lint or invariant.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R85 READ_BACK total cost; R154 financed total + amount-equality invariant; R309 all-in price; R32 Reg Z lint; R231 year-one savings only; R159 cost ranges; R331 union of obligations.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: fee rows are typed mandatory or optional, and a calculator build check folds every mandatory fee (permit, interconnection, HOA, dealer) into total_price. Nothing mandatory may appear only in the envelope. Fragment-sequence invariant: the first price-typed fill of any call must be total_price, and monthly figures may follow only in the same fragment. Per-watt keys are banned from voice. This applies in every profile.

## A10
DEFENSE: The quote is not person-priced. R46 maps configuration to price, "all options to all", with prohibited-basis tags. R288 has one pricing authority and price gaps across arms counted as Tier A, and matched-pair tests prove that equal configurations get equal prices. Usage sets system size, not unit price. The credit tier affects only lender terms, and the lender sets those. R90 already gives an ADMT pre-use notice. Personal data still drives the total, though, and New York counsel may read GBL 349-a to cover it.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R46 product-policy table + matched-pair tests; R288 single pricing authority; R90 ADMT notice; R308 layer stack; R331 traceability matrix; R222 legal attestation; certificate expiry on cited row change.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: the NY layer gets an algorithmic_pricing_disclosure key holding the statutory text verbatim. A co-occurrence lint requires it adjacent to every calculator_output price fill, spoken and written. Each profile attestation also gets an effective_until of at most 30 days, fed by a legislative-change monitor. A new statute, or a lapsed re-attestation, drops that layer to lead-gen with no price until counsel re-attests.
