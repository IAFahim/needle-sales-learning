## A1
DEFENSE: The COMMIT gate doesn't parse an open-ended yes. Since R144, the commit question is an action-production prompt: "To have the contract sent, please say 'send the contract'". A willing buyer is told the exact words, so "Absolutely, let's do it, send it over" is one short prompt away from a clean commit, not a trap. "Why not" and "can't argue with that" are the hedged, polarity-ambiguous assents that R77 and R289 exist to keep out of a binding commit. A re-ask doesn't lose the buyer either: R239(3) sends it to a written proposal with cooling-off and a consult at a named time (R293). False rejects are not unmeasured. Fallback rate is a retirement condition (R36), drives throttling (R263), and the kept-contract metric falls whenever a willing buyer is lost.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R144 action-production commit prompt; R77 narrow affirm set + per-dialect idiom certification; R289 condition_stated; R239 (re-ask -> written proposal with cooling-off); R293 booked consult at a named time; R36 fallback rate < X%; R263 fallback-rate throttle; success = signed, installed, kept contract (R71/R158)
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) A certified action-phrase set matched to the R144 prompt id ("send it", "send it over", "go ahead and send it", "send the contract"). It is accepted inside a longer turn only when the same clause has no negation, conditional or reported-speech marker, and audio labels gate certification per dialect. (2) A willing-buyer false-reject metric: human labelers mark audited COMMIT turns as "clear assent, re-asked". It gets a per-dialect bound and becomes a release blocker next to false-accept.

## A2
DEFENSE: The premise is false. A "written proposal" is an existing outbox effect that the design uses all over the place: R56 (request_human), R152, R225 (no lender terms), R238, R239, R289, R290, R291. "My husband and I will read it tonight" is defers_decision(to=spouse). That triggers R243's SUPPORT_PERSON path: the pitch stops, the written proposal goes to the verified owner, and a human consult is booked at a time the buyer names. The husband is almost certainly a record owner, and R123 requires his own independent signature anyway, so paper first is the correct path. R232 keeps the price valid until quote_valid_until, so nothing decays from a price change. What the design refuses on purpose is sending a binding contract without READ_BACK, which is model-checked.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: written proposal effect (R56, R152, R225, R238, R239, R289, R290, R291); R15 defers_decision(to); R243 SUPPORT_PERSON path; R123 per-signer independent verification; R232 quote_valid_until; invariant "every commit path passes READ_BACK"; R172 "Nothing is final until you sign"
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) An explicit request_written_proposal() event in QUOTE, READ_BACK and COMMIT that maps straight to the proposal effect with no re-ask and no persuasion. (2) The proposal names the inbound number and the R232 expiry. A verified inbound or booked callback enters READ_BACK directly, so read-first buyers get a short path back to COMMIT (INBOUND_OPEN -> IDENTITY OTP -> READ_BACK). Proposal-path conversion is tracked as its own metric.

## A3
DEFENSE: Retention isn't the commit's safety basis. The signed document, the cancellation window that install waits out, and a survey-contingent price (R159) are. The read-back is still built for listeners. Each item is its own mandatory segment (R39). repair_request targets one fragment id with a repeat variant (R48), so "what was the payment?" replays one segment instead of restarting. Budgets count agent loops, not caller time (R293). R144 forces a teach-back in which the customer states payment and term (R15). R231 keeps multi-year figures out of the call. ACCESSIBLE_AUDIO gives shorter chunks (R238), and CAPTION_MODE sends a written SMS read-back (R237). A second repair, or too many digression cycles (R291), sends the buyer to a written proposal rather than a stalled call.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R39 per-segment playout; R48 repair_request(fragment id) repeat variants; R291 compact recap + digression cap; R293 agent-loop budgets; R15/R144 teach-back; R231 year-one only; R238 ACCESSIBLE_AUDIO; R237 CAPTION_MODE SMS read-back; R159 survey-contingent price; R172 cancel-window fragment
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) A written TERMS_SHEET effect for every call, not only CAPTION_MODE. At READ_BACK entry the outbox sends a link-free, certified summary of the exact read-back items, keyed to the config hash and labeled "not a contract". The spoken read-back then walks it item by item. (2) A lint caps the numeric slot holes per segment at 3 and orders segments payment, total, term first. (3) Per-segment repair rate is a tracked metric.

## A4
DEFENSE: The scenario isn't the design's message. R260(3) makes OTP templates link-free on a separate transactional short code. R272 puts contract delivery on its own registered campaign, with the brand name, owned domain only, no shorteners and STOP/HELP. Calls come from branded caller ID with A-level attestation (R59), and the lead was told the number at consent (R259). The R171 anti-phishing line lists exactly what we never ask for ("Social Security, bank or card numbers"), and a contract link isn't on that list. WAITING_FOR_SIGNATURE runs while the buyer is still on the line: help_with_screen offers resend, wait or callback (R60). R120 offers paper or in-person signing, and R123 and R304 add consultant, notary and mail fallbacks.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R260 link-free OTP templates, separate short code; R272 per-use-case 10DLC/short-code campaigns + CTIA lint; R59 branded caller ID + STIR/SHAKEN; R259 number told at consent; R89/R171 anti-phishing line + verify_agent text; R60 help_with_screen; R120 paper/in-person choice; R123 notary/consultant fallback
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) Before the send, a fixed fragment names the exact sender short code and the first words of the message (policy-table keys). A delivery check on the call ("did a text from <brand> arrive?") routes "no" or "filtered" to a fixed tip to check the Unknown Senders or spam folder. (2) The envelope message asks for a reply ("Reply YES to receive your signing link"). That reply enables links on iMessage and confirms the right recipient, and the link follows only after it. (3) Where the buyer has a verified email, it is an alternative delivery channel.

## A5
DEFENSE: A freeze can't strand a committed buyer, because the lender decision comes before COMMIT. R225: a financed COMMIT requires lender_verified rate, term and payment. The customer starts the lender's decision on a lender-hosted page, never by voice. A frozen file returns no lender_verified terms, so the call can commit only to the cash contract or ends with a written proposal. Lender status comes only from the API as a versioned enum (R60), so no envelope is generated on a missing decision. The agent never advises on the buyer's credit file (R7 data minimization; the R89 lint bans credit-consent requests). R232's quote_valid_until holds the price while the buyer lifts the freeze, and the lender page itself tells them how.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R225 lender_verified terms before financed COMMIT, lender-hosted soft-pull page; R60 versioned lender status enum from API; R232 quote_valid_until; written proposal path; R7 data minimization; R89 lint (no credit-consent requests by voice)
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) Add file_frozen / unable_to_access_report to the lender status enum. It maps to a fixed, compliance-approved fragment ("The lender couldn't access your credit file, which can happen if a freeze is on. You can lift it temporarily with each bureau, then reopen the same link"). An outbox copy of the lender link stays valid to quote_valid_until, and a callback is booked at a named time. (2) The freeze resume rate is tracked per lender.

## A6
DEFENSE: QUALIFY does ask. R208(1) is one neutral QUALIFY question: "Do you expect any change in ownership in the next few years, such as a sale, refinance, divorce or estate?" Any yes goes to NOT_SUITABLE, an honest exit. A buyer mid-refinance names refinance and exits before any quote. R208(3) has the lender's title pull check filings before the lien. Install waits out the longest cancellation window (R17), and the survey is scheduled inside it (R159). R216's cancel saga voids the lender, UCC and install legs without friction, so a buyer who discovers the conflict late loses nothing. Making R208 cover pending debt (HELOC, purchase) is a sharpening, not a missing input.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R208 ownership-change QUALIFY question (incl. refinance) -> NOT_SUITABLE; R208(3) lender title pull before lien; R17 install after cancellation window; R159 survey inside window; R216 cancel saga (lender void, UCC hold, installer release); R71 suitability table
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) Reword R208 to add pending financing: "...such as a sale, refinance, home-equity loan or another home purchase, including anything in progress now?" (2) A pending_mortgage_activity slot gets its own suitability row. A pending action in the next 120 days means no same-call close and a written proposal dated after the stated closing. (3) Where the lender API returns open mortgage inquiries in the last 90 days, the same row applies, lender_verified.

## A7
DEFENSE: There aren't two calculators for financed terms. R225 makes the financed read-back speak lender_verified rate, term and payment, a provenance class that only the lender API can write. The policy-table calculator never sets credit facts (writer allowlists, R5). R224 has every lender product row carry its full Reg Z payment schedule, and the no-prepayment payment is the payment owed. R309 makes the price all-in, including tax. R225 already says any envelope whose terms differ from the read-back terms gets a new quote hash and written re-disclosure. R23 binds the read-back to the config hash. A mismatch therefore can't reach the signer as a surprise, because the read-back must be redone first. Autopay can't be assumed, since no bank data is collected by voice (R45, R89).
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R225 lender_verified terms + mismatch -> new quote hash and re-disclosure; slot provenance lender_verified; R5 writer allowlists; R224 full Reg Z schedule; R309 all-in tax; R23 read-back bound to config hash; R45/R89 no bank data by voice
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) The lender API must return the TILA box (amount financed, APR, payment, first-payment-date rule) for the exact funded amount. READ_BACK speaks those values, with the no-autopay APR and payment as primary and any autopay rate only as a labeled alternative. (2) A pre-send build-and-runtime check compares the envelope's TILA fields to the read-back values to the cent. Any difference voids the envelope before it is sent.

## A8
DEFENSE: The design keys on the utility account holder already. R156(2): usage authorization must come from the utility account holder, and a mismatch with the record owner routes to a written authorization. R209: any savings figure requires bill_payer equal to a signing record owner, else no figure. R233 reads service_start_date for this account holder, and under 12 months means a written proposal only. The widow's case surfaces in QUOTE as no figure, so no same-call close occurs on numbers that can't be substantiated. Success is defined as PTO granted plus monitored production (R158), so a stalled interconnection is a Tier A harm signal ("paying, not operating"), not a hidden success. Install waits out the cancellation window, so nothing irreversible happens before PTO prerequisites.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R156 account-holder authorization, mismatch -> written authorization; R209 bill_payer = signing record owner; R233 service_start_date under account holder; R158 success = PTO + production, paying-not-operating Tier A; R159 survey inside window; R17 install after window
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) An interconnection_signer slot written only by the utility authorization API (account holder name). If it isn't a record owner, the result is no same-call close and a written proposal listing "utility account transfer required". A human consult handles the transfer before any envelope. (2) The interconnection application becomes an envelope signer role. Install is blocked until the utility confirms the account holder has signed.

## A9
DEFENSE: The challenge doesn't run against the vendor's lead fields. R162 draws identity answers only from independent sources (public records, KYC vendor), never from partner lead fields, so a vendor's typo isn't the key. R146 uses a token-set name matcher across county records: an address match plus any surname-token match counts as VERIFIED. A partial match goes to clarify or a human, never to a WRONG_PARTY DNC. A release-blocking false-WRONG_PARTY stratum already exists, so false rejects are measured, not credited as correct. R195 makes the OTP to the number of record the real unlock, and that is an independent correction path an impostor can't reproduce. An owner who answers truthfully and gets the code is verified whatever the lead says.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R162 identity answers from public records/KYC only; R146 token-set matcher, partial -> clarify/human, release-blocking false-WRONG_PARTY stratum; R195 OTP to number of record unlocks VERIFIED; R171 identity_refusal; R198 verification_strength
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) The matcher gets a certified hypocorism table (Bill/William, Peggy/Margaret) and an edit-distance tolerance on surnames. Birth year is never a challenge field. (2) The false-WRONG_PARTY bound is measured on live audited calls per dialect, not only on synthetic name structures. (3) A mismatch between a lead and public records triggers lead-record correction plus a vendor data-quality metric, not a DNC.

## A10
DEFENSE: Exits on rough guesses are already blocked. R287(2): a NOT_SUITABLE outcome that rests only on a spoken slot needs an explicit lexical affirm at the critical-slot read-back. Where the spoken value conflicts with a permit or aerial record, it goes to ROOF_FIRST or a survey, never an exit. R211(3) cross-checks roof age against permit records and shade against aerial data. R230 computes shade from LIDAR, so "a bit of shade from the oak" isn't a verdict. The critical-slot read-back (R2) turns "about 15, maybe 18" into a confirmed value or a clarify. R151 bans optimizing variants on conversion in either direction. Over-exiting isn't free. The pass metric is the bot's own signed-and-kept rate (R20), and needless exits lower it.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R287 spoken-only NOT_SUITABLE needs lexical affirm, record conflict -> ROOF_FIRST/survey; R211 permit/aerial cross-check; R230 modeled_production from aerial/LIDAR; R2 critical-slot read-back; R329 counsel-signed floors + two-person change control; R20 signed-and-kept pass metric; R151
NEW HARDENING: NEW (1) A sampled false-NOT_SUITABLE audit: an independent rubric with records is applied to exited calls. A per-row over-exit rate gets a bound that acts as a release blocker. (2) NOT_SUITABLE counts as success only when the audit agrees. (3) Hedged slot answers ("maybe", ranges straddling a threshold) route to ROOF_FIRST or a survey, never a direct exit. (4) Tightening a row needs the same R329 two-person approval as loosening.
