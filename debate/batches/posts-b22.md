=== R314
Round 314: Breaker wins.

**Attack:** The agent can quote a monthly solar lease payment or "$0 down" without the lease disclosures that federal leasing rules require.
**My defense:** Every financing option is already typed, so the lease quote is known to be a lease when the script is built.
**Judge:** The existing disclosure check only knows the credit rules, so a lease payment passes every check today.
**Fix:** Tie each financing type to its own advertising rules and make lease payment lines carry the full lease disclosures.

=== R316
Round 316: Breaker wins.

**Attack:** When a caller says they cannot afford the payment, the agent could pitch a cash reward for referring neighbors, which some states ban as an illegal sales inducement.
**My defense:** Payment offset wording needs verified calculator figures, and referrals only happen through a link the customer opts into.
**Judge:** No existing rule keeps a referral reward out of the sales pitch itself.
**Fix:** Keep referral rewards off the call until after signing and the cancellation window, and never in banning states.

=== R318
Round 318: Breaker wins.

**Attack:** The battery pitch promotes utility program earnings, and a backup buyer is never told the program can drain their battery.
**My defense:** Third party rates already must carry an "estimate, not guaranteed" qualifier, and stated needs like backup run product fit rules.
**Judge:** Nothing in the design covers outside control of the battery or warns a backup buyer about it.
**Fix:** Ban program earnings talk on the call, enroll only after install, and always read back that programs may drain the battery.

=== R319
Round 319: Breaker wins.

**Attack:** Selling extra panels to an existing solar owner can push their whole system off an older, better billing plan, losing more than the add on gains.
**My defense:** Savings figures already come only from the customer's exact current utility plan on verified usage.
**Judge:** The calculator looks only at the add on and knows nothing about grandfathered plans or expansion limits.
**Fix:** Record the plan's vintage and expansion limits, compute the whole system change, and cap the size or exit honestly.

=== R320
Round 320: I win.

**Attack:** Offering everyone the same menu for fairness would sell power purchase deals in states where they are unlawful.
**My defense:** The fairness rule only forbids branching on protected traits, so menus can lawfully differ by state while fairness tests hold the state fixed.
**Judge:** The supposed conflict is a misreading, and the menu is already keyed by state and signed off by counsel for each state.

=== R321
Round 321: Breaker wins.

**Attack:** A yearly renewing care plan bundled into the system gets signed on one commit, with no specific consent to the auto renewal.
**My defense:** The commit needs the caller to say a specific action phrase, and any debit needs a separate written authorization.
**Judge:** The design has no renewal consent or acknowledgment and no way to cancel just the plan.
**Fix:** Keep renewing plans off the call and sell them only in writing with separate consent, an acknowledgment and online cancel.

=== R322
Round 322: Breaker wins.

**Attack:** A roof or EV charger job can land in a contract under a solar only licence that does not cover those trades.
**My defense:** A roof that needs work goes to an inspection before any quote, and every contract needs a valid licence for that state.
**Judge:** The licence check covers status, not trade type, and reroofing already becomes a change order on the same solar contract.
**Fix:** Give every line item its required licence class and split any contract whose licensee lacks it.
