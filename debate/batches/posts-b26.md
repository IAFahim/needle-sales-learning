=== R354

**Attack:** The commit step rejects natural, enthusiastic yeses like "absolutely, send it over", so the most eager buyers get asked again and drift off.
**My defense:** The agent already tells the buyer the exact words to say to have the contract sent, so a willing buyer is one clear instruction away from a clean commit.
**Judge:** The attack describes an old yes or no question, and a hedged "why not" is exactly what should not count as binding consent.

=== R355

**Judge:** The premise is wrong, since read first buyers get paper and a held price, and only the binding contract waits for the read back.

=== R356

=== R360

**Attack:** The payment the agent reads out comes from its own calculator while the signed loan documents come from the lender's, so the numbers differ at signing.
