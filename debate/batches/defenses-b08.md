## A1
DEFENSE: The agent never sets credit terms. "Financing (lender runs application and decisions)" means the creditor makes the covered-borrower determination at prequal, which is where the MLA puts it. Terms come back as "lender_verified" status, and "conditional approval at new amount invalidates quote hash" (R60). The read-back is bound to that hash (R23/R49), so the agent cannot read a non-MLA menu once the lender flags the borrower. "Same menu for all" covers offers only. R46 keys the product policy on non-protected facts, and a legally required term is one of those. The hedged horizon is weak: "two years, maybe sooner" is conditional speech. Under R81 that wording stays out of commits, and R43 range checks send it to clarify.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: Compliance gates "financing (lender runs application and decisions, Reg Z trigger-term lint)"; R60 lender status enum and quote-hash invalidation; R23/R49 read-back bound to quote hash; R46 product-policy table on non-protected keys; R79/R90 credit band only from lender prequal; R151 lender contract price in READ_BACK; R43 numeric ranges -> clarify; R71 move-horizon suitability slot.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: The lender API must return covered_borrower (from its MLA database check) as a lender_verified slot before QUOTE. If it is true: (a) the MLA contract template applies, with no mandatory arbitration and no waiver clauses (build check); (b) the calculator computes MAPR including the dealer fee, and if MAPR exceeds 36% the product is NOT_SUITABLE or cash-only; (c) READ_BACK adds the policy-table MLA oral disclosure fragment (MAPR statement plus payment obligation). Hedge markers on move_horizon ("probably", "maybe sooner", "till my orders come") clamp the slot to its lower bound, and military-relocation phrasings map to the shortest horizon row.

## A2
DEFENSE: The bankruptcy does not stay hidden. Financed deals need a lender pull ("lender runs application and decisions"), and an open Chapter 13 shows on any credit file. The lender then returns declined or conditional, a "versioned status enum" event that the agent cannot override (R60). R88 already sends "capacity flags (conservatorship/trust/POA) from records and lender checks -> human review path", and no lien filing proceeds until R120 verification is done. The R147 hardship lexicon covers "foreclosure, collections". Saying "payments are fine" does not stop a deterministic lexicon hit on "Chapter 13" if that row exists. The real gap is narrower: cash deals, and the fact that the spoken words are lost.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: Financing compliance gate (lender decisions); R60 lender status events; R88 capacity flags -> human review; R147 hardship lexicon rows and red-team stratum; R19 sensitive_event(financial_hardship) "no pitch on hardship"; R120 no lien/install until owners verified; R138 mailed notice before install.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: Add bankruptcy lexicon rows ("chapter 7/11/13", "bankruptcy", "trustee", "plan payments") that fire sensitive_event(financial_hardship, legal_capacity): no pitch, then human review. Add a bankruptcy_estate capacity flag from a PACER/public-record check on every record owner, run at record-owner lookup and again before any lien/UCC or install effect, for cash deals too. If the flag is set, there is no close and no filing without an uploaded trustee consent or court order, which a human verifies.

## A3
DEFENSE: The son cannot complete the contract. The signer list is "all record owners" from "record-owner lookup on validated address" (R88), and that is Dorothy, not whoever answered the phone. R159 requires "lender-grade identity verification (document plus selfie or KBA) for each record owner at e-sign", and R120 requires "per-signer vendor ID verification" with no shared phones. Family knowledge can pass the call's challenge, but it cannot produce Dorothy's ID with a matching selfie. R138 mails the contract to every record owner, and install is blocked until then. The deceased list is a pre-dial gate. I accept that R143's "address plus any surname-token match counts as VERIFIED" lets a son pass on the call. That is a gap, and the commit gates catch it.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R19/pre-dial deceased list; R88 record-owner lookup, signer list, capacity flags -> human review; R120 per-signer independent verification, no lien/install until each owner is verified; R159 document+selfie per record owner, independent identity sources; R138 mailed notice, install blocked; R143 name matcher.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: VERIFIED binds the speaker to a specific record-owner name. A surname-only match marks the speaker as a household member, and the non-owner path applies (no close). Record-owner lookup also checks death-index and probate filings. "It's ours", "my mom's", "the estate" or "family home" phrasing from a non-owner, or any deceased hit, sets a capacity flag (estate). The outcome is no close and a human review that needs letters testamentary plus the jurisdiction's notice or court authority.

## A4
DEFENSE: Install does not happen on the read-back's terms if the design changes. R156 schedules the survey "inside the cancellation window" and makes the contract "contingent on a survey-confirmed price". It also says "every change order becomes a new quote hash with written re-disclosure, a fresh e-sign by all signers, a no-penalty exit and a restart of the cancellation window". An HOA-forced relocation that changes size or production is that kind of change. R157's WAITING_FOR_BOM_MATCH blocks install on any config mismatch. On the legal question, policy rows are jurisdiction-keyed ("jurisdiction profiles", "most restrictive wins"). A row worded as if it held in every state violates R91's rule that each fact maps to one table key.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R156 survey-contingent contract, change order = new quote hash + re-sign + window restart; R157 WAITING_FOR_BOM_MATCH; R23 read-back bound to config hash; regulation profiles (R47/R70); R91 fact tokens = table keys; R154 "assumption" qualifier and no figure without exact inputs.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: Add an hoa slot to QUALIFY, sourced from the deed-restriction/HOA record lookup on the validated address and confirmed by speech. If an HOA applies, READ_BACK must state "subject to HOA approval; size, layout and savings may change", and the HOA application must be filed inside the cancellation window. Any HOA-mandated change counts as an R156 change order. Add per-state hoa_solar_rights rows (TX 202.010, CA 714). A lint bans unkeyed HOA-rights answers. If no row exists for that state, the reply is "we'll confirm in writing".

## A5
DEFENSE: The design already treats seller-side legal status as a live, expiring guard. R119 does this for telemarketer registration ("no DIAL and no SMS effect without valid registration", with an expiry alert). R158's "counterparty-health monitor (bankruptcy filings, covenant breach, lender watch-lists, missed-milestone rates)" watches the installer. R137 makes the policy table forward-only, with tombstones no bundle can cite, and "the runtime refuses any row outside its effective_from/until window". So a licence row with a short validity window simply stops being citable, and QUOTE/READ_BACK fall to lead-gen or callback. I accept that today the licence row has no live feed behind it. That part is NEW.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R119 seller_registration guard + invariant; R158 counterparty-health monitor and named warranty backers; R137 forward-only table, tombstones, effective-window refusal, fallback to lead-gen; certificates expire when a cited row changes; R160 independent outcome feeds.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: installer_license rows per jurisdiction (CSLB, AZ ROC, FL DBPR and others) are fed by an automated daily pull of board status plus workers'-comp and bond certificate feeds. Each row's effective_until is at most 24 hours past its last successful check, and a stale feed fails closed. Model-checked invariant: no QUOTE, READ_BACK, CONTRACT_SENT or install effect unless that installer's row is valid for the property's jurisdiction. The row is re-checked at install. A lapse after signing holds install, sends written notice and offers a no-penalty cancel.

## A6
DEFENSE: The agent cannot improvise any of the 489.147 violations. "Caller free text never spoken". Every utterance is "a human-approved fragment", and the banned-claims lint plus "lint bans fake urgency and unverifiable external-fact fragments (e.g. weather)" (R78) mean no fragment can mention storms, coverage or deductibles. A coverage question has no tool in QUOTE/QUALIFY, so it becomes out_of_scope -> clarify, then fallback. R78 also suppresses emergency counties for "declaration + cool-down" and audits emergency-area closes as Tier A. ROOF_FIRST says "inspection before any quote". It is a suitability exit, not a claims offer. Still, a roofer referral in a storm state needs a profile row.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: Speech = certified fragments, no caller free text; R18 banned-claims lint; R78 emergency suppression + cool-down, weather/urgency lint, Tier A audit; R1/R82 out_of_scope with honest no-offer fragment; R71 ROOF_FIRST; regulation profiles, most restrictive wins.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: Add an out_of_scope(insurance_claim) event with one fixed fragment: "We can't discuss insurance claims or coverage; please contact your insurer." A lint bans insurance, claim, deductible, adjuster and storm-damage tokens in all fragments. Storm-state profile rows (FL 489.147, TX Ins. 707 and others) turn ROOF_FIRST during a post-declaration window into a neutral written "roof must be addressed first" exit, with no partner-roofer referral or assessment offer. The case is suppressed from redial until the window closes.

## A7
DEFENSE: R147 already decides this: "PACE products are excluded or follow ability-to-repay per the regulation profile." The default is exclusion. A PACE administrator can be offered only with its own profile, so it cannot ride on generic loan fragments. "Pay through my taxes" names a product the menu doesn't hold, so it maps to out_of_scope(product_request), "honest no-offer fragment and referral" (R82). "It's a government program" is barred twice: by the banned-claims lint ("government affiliation", R18) and by "lint bans utility/government pretexts" (R31). A deductibility answer would need a policy-table row with substantiation (R91), and none is authored. So the agent cannot say yes or no.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R147 PACE exclusion/ability-to-repay; R82 out_of_scope(product_request); R18/R31 government-affiliation lints; R91 every fact token a substantiated table key; R32 Reg Z lint; R151 lender contract price.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: A build check types every financing row (loan, lease, PPA, PACE). Any PACE row fails the build unless a full PACE profile exists: DFPI licensing, recorded oral confirmation call, ability-to-pay, annual assessment and lien-priority read-back items, and the CFPB 2024 TILA rule. A lint bans tax-deductibility and tax-benefit wording in all fragments except a fixed "consult a tax professional".

## A8
DEFENSE: Each of these failures is stopped by an existing fail-closed input. R88's record-owner lookup on a trust homesite returns no fee owner, or the United States in trust, and that is a capacity case routed to human review, not a signer list. R154 says "the calculator must use the customer's exact tariff schedule ... else no figure". NTUA has no tariff row, so no savings are spoken. R153 makes verified usage come only from a utility API or bill extractor. The lender makes the lending decision and will not secure on a leasehold without BIA approval. R120 says "no lien filing or install proceeds until every owner has been independently verified". The missing tribal jurisdiction layer is a real gap.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R88 record-owner lookup + capacity flags; R154 exact tariff or no figure; R153 verified_usage writers; R61/R72 savings only from calculator_output; financing gate (lender decisions); R120 lien gating; R70 candidate jurisdiction set.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: The jurisdiction model gains a tribal layer (BIA land-area and Census AIANNH boundaries, checked on the validated address). An address inside trust or reservation land loads a tribal profile. With no certified tribal profile, the call runs lead-gen only (R47 pattern), goes to a human consultant, and has no lien or UCC product. Utilities not in the tariff table (NTUA, co-ops, tribal utilities) force no figure.

## A9

XVI §50(a)(5) row: the 5-day wait, the closing venue, and the consent of a spouse who is not on title.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R120 per-signer verification, notary/in-person fallback for liens; R117 paper/in-person route; R47 lead-gen mode by jurisdiction; R88 signer list; R151 contract amount invariant; regulation profiles, most restrictive wins; R138 install blocked pending notice.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: A TX homestead profile row, keyed on the county appraisal homestead exemption. Any lien or UCC product on such a home makes the call lead-gen only. The contract closes only at a title company, lender or attorney office. The trigger is a title-company API event, with a 5-day timer from the application timestamp, both spouses signing (including a non-titled spouse), and a 3-day rescission. Model-checked invariant: no lien-filing effect without the closing_venue event. Cash or no-lien products may still e-sign.

## A10
DEFENSE: Before dialing, the gate uses the best available knowledge: "calling hours ... for the most restrictive candidate jurisdiction". It pins tzdata and adds guard bands (R136). Once the caller states a location, R70 says jurisdiction_changed "fired on any stated residence change, validated or not; unvalidated -> treat as added candidate" and "re-evaluates hour ... guards -> WRAP_UP + lawful callback". The install fear is answered by R156 (survey inside the cancellation window, needing the owner), R138 (mailed notice, install blocked), and R117 (install waits for verified delivery). The gap: "I'm in Portugal" is a current location, not a residence, and it can be labeled small_talk.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: Pre-dial most-restrictive candidate jurisdiction gate; R136 pinned tzdata, guard bands; R70 jurisdiction_changed -> WRAP_UP + lawful callback; R41 temporal resolver; R156 survey inside cancellation window; R138 mailed notice; R117 delivery verification; R135 per-number dial permit.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: A GLOBAL current_location event (lexicon plus Needle: "I'm in/abroad/travelling", "it's the middle of the night", "you woke me") with a certified small_talk confusion pair. It adds the stated place's time zone to the candidate set. If that time is outside the window, the call apologizes and ends with a callback booked in the caller's stated local time. It also writes a number-level away_until hold into the dial permit, so later campaign waves do not dial. Any stated away-from-home means no same-call close, and the contract waits until the owner confirms they are back.
