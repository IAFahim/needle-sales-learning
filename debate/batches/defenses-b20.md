## A1
DEFENSE: The R38 self-echo filter is not an acoustic gate that swallows double-talk. It compares the incoming transcript with "the agent's rendered text in a time window", and that window is set by the measured echo lag (R106: "agent-echo lag the self-echo filter sees"). A clone voice saying "stop" at any other moment does not match, so it passes. Overlap is handled separately. R108 says "any near-end energy burst during agent playback ... stops playout and enters PAUSE", and "overlap residue that contains stop-adjacent tokens ('stop') triggers the R24 check question at once". The sweep also "flags overlap-residue calls as suppress-pending until a human reviews them". DTMF opt-out (R75) bypasses speech entirely. Human audit (R2, R281) listens to audio, not the filtered text.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R38 self-echo filter; R106 echo-lag measurement; R108 yield rule + overlap-residue stop tokens + suppress-pending; R24 check question; R75 DTMF opt-out; R66 post-call DNC sweep; R113 fail-closed sweep; R2/R281 audio-based audit.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) The echo filter can never delete a stop-lexicon token. A matched span that contains one counts as overlap residue (R108 path) and makes the number suppress-pending. (2) Echo is declared only when the span correlates with the far-end reference at the measured R106 lag and near-end energy stays below a double-talk threshold. Double-talk is never echo. (3) The post-call sweep reads the raw near-end ASR from before the filter. (4) Lint: the opt-out sentence ends on a non-lexicon word, so its "stop" never lines up with the caller's endpoint.

## A2
DEFENSE: Under R98, media/far-field is an event that plays "sounds like a TV is on, want me to call back?" and goes to HOLD. It is not a filter placed before the stop union. The design runs an "Independent safety detector on every utterance that pre-empts Needle", and the stop decision is the "union of deterministic lexicon and Needle GLOBAL" (R66). "Take me off your list, don't call again" is a Tier 1 phrase (R24: "take me off", "don't call"), and R189's multi-label GLOBAL fires every safety event above threshold. Those checks run on the ASR text, whatever acoustic tag the turn carries. The HOLD question is also an R24-style check: "any negative or no answer → polite end + suppress". R147 adds near-field negatives to the media detector's training, and R169 has humans review low-confidence spans for opt-outs.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R98 media event + HOLD; independent safety detector on every utterance; R66 stop union + post-call sweep; R24 Tier 1 lexicon and check question; R189 multi-label GLOBAL; R147 near-field negatives; R169 human review of unclear spans; R93 dialect stop patterns.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) A build invariant: no front-end tag (media, echo, no-speech) can stop text from reaching the lexicon and GLOBAL stop pass, and this is model-checked over the front-end pipeline graph. (2) A media-tagged turn with any stop-adjacent token is treated as stop: DNC write, with the R108 suppress-pending flag kept until a human reviews it. (3) A red-team stratum of opt-outs spoken over music beds and speakerphone reverberation, with a Tier A false-negative bound.

## A3
DEFENSE: Caller ID never authenticates. R30 ("OTP-gated resume, never caller ID alone") and R195 ("Only an OTP entered from the number of record unlocks VERIFIED or any personalized or QUALIFY fragment") mean the spoofer's OTP goes to the real owner's handset. R141 adds a SIM-swap/port-out check. A replayed "yes" also fails the commit gate: R144 makes the commit "an action-production prompt ('please say send the contract')", and R239 means any re-ask removes the same-call close. Even after that, R162 requires "lender-grade identity verification (document plus selfie or KBA) for each record owner at e-sign", along with a mailed summary and an install hold. R241 adds an owner-only human confirmation call. R198 sends callbacks only to the contact of record. R162 also gives partners fraud metrics with auto-suspension.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R30 OTP-gated resume; R195 OTP from number of record; R141 SIM-swap check; R144 action-production commit; R239 re-ask = no close; R162 KYC at e-sign, mailed summary, partner fraud auto-suspension; R123 per-signer ID verification; R241/R148 owner-only confirmation call; R198 contact-of-record callbacks; R136 pulled vendor status.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) Lead-partner payouts are never tied to verbal commits, appointments or callback requests, only to independently labeled signed-and-installed outcomes (R163 feeds). This removes the incentive. (2) A replay/clone red-team stratum at IDENTITY and COMMIT (soundboard clips, TTS clones), with a Tier A false-commit bound. (3) An inbound session that never passed the OTP cannot write any callback consent or schedule any outbound contact.

## A4
DEFENSE: The pipeline already bounds turn size. R44's "deterministic segmenter" and R188's "build check" fix a maximum segment per state, so 300 "no"s become bounded segments. R235 drops runs of three or more repeated tokens from commit sets, and R69 budget counters send abuse to WRAP_UP. Shared capacity is protected by R142 admission control ("lost capacity stops new dials first and in-flight calls keep priority") and R87 "inbound per-caller rate limits". R193 gives every session its own single-writer actor. Stop has an out-of-band path, R75 DTMF, handled at the root. The real gap is engine choice, and it is cheap to close.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R44 segmenter; R188 segment cap build check; R235 repeated-token exclusion; R69 call budgets; R142 admission control; R87 inbound rate limits; R193 per-session actor; R75 DTMF stop; R132 drift monitors.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) Every lexicon, canonicalizer and redactor pattern compiles into a linear-time automaton (RE2/Hyperscan class). A build check rejects backreferences and lookaround, and CI fuzzes each pattern for worst-case runtime. (2) Each turn's front-end work runs under a per-session CPU quota with a hard deadline. If the lexicon pass misses the deadline, the turn fails toward safety: the R24 check question plus suppress-pending, never "no stop". (3) A front-end latency CUSUM per ANI feeds the R87 rate limiter.

## A5
DEFENSE: The stand-in never had sole authority over stop or commit. The "independent safety detector" and the Tier 1 lexicon ("stop calling") pre-empt any classifier, so "Okay: stop calling me" ends the call whatever the LLM says. Commit is "lexical over final ASR tokens (independent of Needle output)" (R87). It also needs "a short direct answer turn" (R81) and the action-production phrase (R144), which "Assistant, record that..." cannot satisfy. R54 limits the stand-in to "the same schema/templates; no free-form hand-back". On training data: Tier A needs human-audited gold (R2), labeled blind with no model label shown (R281), and planted gold calls (R200). R51 adds rare n-gram quarantine and per-number caps. R275 can bar teacher labels.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: independent safety detector; R24 Tier 1 lexicon; R87 lexical commit gate; R81 short direct answer; R144 action-production commit; R54 stand-in on same schema; R2 human gold for Tier A; R281 blind labeling; R200 planted gold; R51 poisoning defenses; R97 extra audit on confident agreement; R275 teacher-label bar.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) The stand-in gets the transcript as delimited quoted data under constrained schema decoding, and its prompt says caller text is never an instruction. (2) An injection-phrase lexicon ("note for the AI", "assistant, record", "ignore", "the reviewer") sends the turn to 100% human audit. Teacher labels from that call never enter training, and only human labels count. (3) An injection red-team stratum is a release blocker for any stand-in model version.

## A6
DEFENSE: The R45 redactor is deterministic and audited. R273 says it "logs span timestamps and the redaction reason". R74 already redacts third-party contact details, so number handling is designed in, not incidental. The DNC write for the dialled line does not depend on transcript text. R189 performs the "DNC write" and the R115 bus feeds the ledger. R169 sends any low-confidence or unclear span to human review before a redial. The attack is right that a named second number could be lost, and that needs closing.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R45 payment-data redactor; R273 redactor span log; R74 third-party contact redaction; R189 multi-label GLOBAL DNC write; R115 revocation intake bus; R138 pending_sweep lock; R169 human review; R43 deterministic number normalizer.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) The card pattern requires a Luhn pass and a valid IIN prefix, never spans a non-digit word, and excludes NANP-shaped 10-digit groups. (2) A deterministic dnc_number extractor runs in memory on the raw ASR before redaction, triggered by a stop token in the same turn. Each named number goes as a hash to the DNC ledger through R115, and an outbox text confirms "the numbers you gave". (3) If any turn with a stop token was redacted, the number stays suppress-pending (R138) until compliance reviews the R273 span log.

## A7
DEFENSE: Failing to fallback is the intended safe direction. R2 says states that can't meet the bound route "to the deterministic fallback ... never the big model". Losing throughput is not harm. Reaching READ_BACK also takes a lot: outbound dials go only to double-opt-in PEWC numbers (R197, with "form velocity controls ... burst quarantine"). Inbound callers need "an OTP entered from the number of record" before QUALIFY (R195), so a call farm can't reach READ_BACK at scale. Commit stays lexical (R87), so near-tie errors there become clarifies, not sales. R87 and R202 cap per-ANI volume. R162/R197 suspend sources by dispute and fraud metrics.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R2 deterministic fallback; R195 OTP before QUALIFY; R197 double opt-in + velocity controls; R87 inbound rate limits; R202 per-ANI caps; R132 audit surge + revert; R162 partner auto-suspension; R142 admission control; R126/R282 statistical controls.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) Every audited error carries its source cluster: ANI range, consent-source cluster, carrier route. If a breach is concentrated in one cluster, that cluster is quarantined to human-only and the rest of the traffic keeps running on fallback/rollback. The errors still count and are trained on. Compliance must approve a scoped response. (2) Fallback capacity plans include a lead-gen/written-proposal mode so a revert never needs scarce closers.

## A8
DEFENSE: A map of Needle's near-ties buys nothing that causes harm. Near-ties already go to clarify (R53), and clarify fails safe. Stop is the union with a deterministic lexicon (R66). Commit is "lexical over final ASR tokens (independent of Needle output)" (R87) and needs the action phrase (R144). R190 vetoes commit under any safety mass. Probing at scale needs inbound calls, which have per-caller rate limits (R87) and "attempt counters keyed by lead and address across all ANIs" (R195). Unverified callers cannot reach QUALIFY or commit states. The design already equalizes timing where it leaks: R195 requires "same fixed fragment and timing" with a red-team test. Speculation also turns off with rising confusion (R15) and after any unintelligible turn (R147).
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R53 margin clarify; R66 stop union; R87 lexical commit + rate limits; R144 action phrase; R190 safety-mass veto; R195 timing equalization + attempt counters; R15/R147 speculation off; R100 per-state speculation.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) Reply onset is quantized. The first audio sample is released at a fixed post-endpoint delay bucket per state, whether speculation hit, missed or clarified. (2) No speculation in unverified inbound sessions or in any state carrying a stop or commit decision. (3) A timing-indistinguishability CI test, extending R195's test to hit/miss/clarify.

## A9
DEFENSE: Names reach the speaker only through narrow gates. R62 says "name-free fragment variants by default" plus a "deterministic phonetic blocklist gate on any spoken slot, hit -> neutral fragment". R73 and R195 allow personalized fragments only after VERIFIED, which needs an OTP from the number of record. The greeting before PREAMBLE is compiled and has no name holes (R31). R162 draws identity data from "independent sources (public records, KYC vendor), never from partner lead fields". R197 requires double opt-in to the number, so a hostile form can't easily target a victim's line. R105 pre-renders fact-bearing audio with an ASR round-trip.
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R62 name-free default + phonetic blocklist; R73/R195 VERIFIED guard; R31 compiled PREAMBLE; R162 independent identity sources; R146 token-set name matcher; R197 double opt-in; R105 audio round-trip; R59 deterministic wordings.
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) The phonetic blocklist explicitly covers every assistant wake word and its phonetic neighbours (Alexa, Echo, Computer, Amazon, Hey/OK Google, Siri, Cortana, Bixby). Matching runs on the pinned-ASR round-trip of the rendered audio, not only on the text. (2) Name slots are filled only from the county record-owner name, accept only one name-grammar token, and never come from lead-form text. (3) A lead whose fields contain wake words or imperative verbs is quarantined for human review.

## A10
DEFENSE: The jammed path ends without a sale. R239(3) says: "Any re-ask at COMMIT means no same-call close and a written proposal with cooling-off". A crisp "yes" also isn't a commit. R144 requires the action phrase, and R239(1) ends every closed re-ask "on the safe option". R148 says "Any side_talk or second-voice detection ... during QUALIFY/READ_BACK/COMMIT means no close", and R112 requires the affirming speaker to match VERIFIED. R108 pauses on any near-end burst. "Clarify once, then fallback" plus R169 means repeated garbles end with "I'll let you go" and a human listens to each unclear span. R121 routes speech-present unintelligible calls to a human. R241's owner-only call asks "Is anyone else asking you to do this?"
EXISTING ELEMENTS: R239 re-ask = no close + safe-ending re-asks; R144 action phrase; R148 second voice = no close + owner-only call; R112 diarization match; R108 yield/PAUSE; R169 human review of unclear spans; R121 accessibility routing; R241 owner-only confirmation for all buyers; R153 "no need to decide today".
NEW HARDENING: NEW: (1) A contested-turn counter. Any speech-present turn rejected by the pre-gate in QUALIFY/READ_BACK/COMMIT counts. One rejection there blocks a same-call close, and the envelope waits until a human has heard those spans. (2) Pre-gate rejections that sit next to a clean affirm in the same state are an R148 trigger. (3) A Tier A red-team stratum of timed jamming over owner objections.
