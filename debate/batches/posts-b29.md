=== R384
Round 384: I win.

- **Attack:** California law says a robocall machine may play recorded audio only after a live human introduces the call and asks permission.
- **My defense:** Every number is dialed only with that person's written consent, and nothing outside a certified state profile ever gets dialed.
- **Judge:** California law treats prior consent as an alternative to the live introduction and exempts non-random calls to people who asked for them, so the consent gate already covers this.

=== R385
Round 385: Breaker wins.

- **Attack:** Every artificial voice call must state a callback number, and the opening never says one.
- **My defense:** The number shows on caller ID and is given whenever asked.
- **Judge:** The rule is a duty on every call, and giving the number only on request or on screen doesn't meet it.
- **Fix:** Add a staffed callback number as a required opening segment, repeat it at every agent hang-up, and prove per call that it played.

=== R386
Round 386: I win.

- **Attack:** A "9 in 10 customers are happy" claim built only from satisfied installed customers is biased review data the FTC can punish.
- **My defense:** Social proof can only be an audited table fact with substantiation, quantifiers need a table entry, and counsel reviews the overall impression.
- **Judge:** A biased sample would fail the required substantiation audit, and the design has no such satisfaction claim in the first place.

=== R387
Round 387: Breaker wins.

- **Attack:** If the lender's loan papers omit the FTC notice letting borrowers raise seller claims against the lender, or cap it, the seller breaks the rule.
- **My defense:** The notice is already a required, read-back clause in the contract template.
- **Judge:** Nothing checks the lender's own loan document for the exact notice or bans a recovery cap.
- **Fix:** Admit only lender documents with the exact notice and no cap, and refuse contracts or funding on any other version.

=== R388

- **Attack:** Lead lists filtered by credit score or overnight lender soft pulls are credit reports used for marketing without a firm offer of credit.

=== R389
Round 389: I win.

- **Attack:** Handing callers to partner roofers, battery programs or partner installers is a second seller mid-call with no new seller disclosure.
- **My defense:** The roof outcome only books an inspection, transfers go to the company's own staff, and third-party programs are offered later in writing under their own terms.
- **Judge:** No path in the design hands a live caller to a different seller, and partner data sharing is already gated.

=== R390
Round 390: I win.

- **Attack:** A text confirming the opt-out must be a single plain message sent within five minutes, and the outbox can send it late with branding and links.
- **My defense:** Every text template is certified with no promotional wording, and marketing messages are dropped the moment someone says stop.
- **Judge:** The scenario's branded content can't be sent, and a late plain confirmation isn't automatically unlawful under the FCC's rule.

=== R391
Round 391: Breaker wins.

- **Attack:** Florida and similar states require specific consent form wording, and the dial check only confirms the seller and AI voice are named.
- **My defense:** The exact consent text each lead saw is stored, and state rules already sit in the dial check.
- **Judge:** Nothing compares that stored text against each state's required wording, and Florida lets people sue for $500 per call.
- **Fix:** Have counsel approve each consent text against every state's wording and dial nobody whose text isn't approved.

=== R392
Round 392: Breaker wins.

- **Attack:** Saying a monthly or per-watt price before the full total breaks Massachusetts and California junk fee rules.
- **My defense:** The total cost is a required read-back item and the contract price is stated all-in.
- **Judge:** Nothing controls which price is spoken first or keeps mandatory fees out of the contract packet alone.
- **Fix:** Fold every mandatory fee into the total, make the total the first price spoken on any call, and ban per-watt figures from voice.

=== R393
Round 393: Breaker wins.

- **Attack:** New York requires a notice that a price was set by an algorithm using personal data, and the agent never says it.
- **My defense:** Prices come from one fixed table by configuration, and every buyer gets the same options.
- **Judge:** The New York definition covers a total computed from the household's usage, roof and address.
- **Fix:** Speak and print the exact New York notice with every calculated price, and stop quoting wherever rules change until counsel signs off.
