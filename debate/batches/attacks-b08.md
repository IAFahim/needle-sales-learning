## A1
TOPIC: Active-duty military buyers: Military Lending Act terms and PCS-order move horizons the uniform "same menu for all" financing cannot handle
SCENARIO: A Navy petty officer in Norfolk answers. QUALIFY asks move horizon; caller says "we're here till my orders come, probably two years, maybe sooner." Needle labels move_horizon with grounded "two years", which passes the table. QUOTE reads the standard loan menu (dealer fee inside the price, arbitration clause, standard APR) in READ_BACK. Caller says "yes." Contract goes out by SMS. Nothing in the graph asks about military status, and nothing changes the terms read back.
WHY IT BREAKS: MLA (32 CFR 232.6, 232.8) requires covered-borrower checks, an oral MAPR and payment disclosure, a 36% all-in MAPR cap, and no mandatory arbitration. A loan breaking these rules is void from the start. The design's fair-lending rule ("same menu for all") hard-codes the terms a covered borrower cannot get. And the vague "till my orders come" answer passes a numeric move-horizon guard. Sources: ecfr.gov 32 CFR 232.6; FDIC Compliance Manual V-13.1.

## A2
TOPIC: Homeowner in an active Chapter 13 bankruptcy, legally barred from taking on new debt or liens without trustee or court approval
SCENARIO: The caller says calmly, "We're in a Chapter 13, payments are fine, so money's not an issue." Needle has no bankruptcy event. It picks acknowledge or small_talk (the closest allowed labels), and the payments-are-fine framing never triggers the vulnerability counter. Ownership, roof and shade all pass. The lender's soft pull is ordered. The caller affirms the read-back. A UCC fixture filing is recorded against a house that belongs to the bankruptcy estate.
WHY IT BREAKS: Under 11 U.S.C. 1306, the house and the debtor's post-petition income belong to the bankruptcy estate. New credit needs trustee or court approval (11 U.S.C. 1305(c), 364, and local rules). Unapproved debt can cause plan default or dismissal. The suitability table checks physical and financial facts, not legal capacity. The closed event schema has no label for a court-supervised debtor, so the fact is lost.

## A3
TOPIC: Probate and estate-owned homes: an heir passes the identity challenge and commits a house titled to a dead person
SCENARIO: The lead record names Dorothy. Dorothy died eight months ago, and the deceased list has not caught up. Her son, who lives in the house, answers her landline. The challenge-response (address, name on the account, year bought) is family knowledge, so he passes as VERIFIED. "Do you own the home?" "Yeah, it's ours." Grief is long past, so no sensitive_event fires. The e-sign goes to "all record owners," which is the contact on file: the son's mobile.
WHY IT BREAKS: Title sits with the estate. A personal representative can encumber estate property only with court authority or the statutory notice procedure (e.g., California Probate Code 10514 notice of proposed action under independent administration). An heir has no authority at all before distribution. Knowledge-based identity checks fail by design against family members. Ownership is a spoken yes/no slot, not a check of title or probate status.

## A4
TOPIC: HOA architectural approval and state-specific solar-access law: approval arrives after the cancellation window and moves or shrinks the system
SCENARIO: A Texas homeowner in a deed-restricted subdivision affirms a 9 kW south-and-street-facing design. QUALIFY never asks about an HOA. Install waits out the 3-day window, then the HOA application goes in. Under its rules the HOA moves the panels to the rear, west-facing roof. If the customer instead asks "Can my HOA stop this?", the policy table holds one answer, worded as true for every state.
WHY IT BREAKS: Tex. Prop. Code 202.010 lets an HOA require approval and dictate panel location, as long as estimated production drops no more than 10%. California Civil Code 714 gives HOAs a 45-day review. Either way, the size, production and savings read back can legally change after the commit, once cancellation has expired. HOA law differs by state, and no suitability row or read-back item covers it.

## A5
TOPIC: Contractor licence status changing daily at state licensing boards (suspensions, bond or workers' comp lapses) outside the certified policy table
SCENARIO: The policy table names partner installer "CSLB #XXXXXX, licensed, bonded and insured," and the PREAMBLE/READ_BACK fragments cite it. On Tuesday the CSLB automatically suspends the licence for a workers' compensation lapse. The certificate expires only when a cited policy row changes, and nobody changed the row. For two weeks the agent reads "licensed" to every Californian, closes 300 deals, and the installer performs them.
WHY IT BREAKS: Under B&P 7031, unlicensed work can't be compensated, and the customer can recover everything paid. Suspension for a workers' comp lapse is automatic (B&P 7125.2). B&P 7159 requires accurate licence details in the contract. Arizona ROC, Florida DBPR and others act on their own schedules. Licence status is an external, live legal fact. The pre-dial gate checks the consumer side only, never the seller's legal right to perform.

## A6
TOPIC: Post-hurricane ROOF_FIRST outcomes colliding with Florida's ban on contractors soliciting insurance-claim roof work
SCENARIO: The county emergency suppression lifts after a hurricane. A Tampa homeowner: "Roof's damaged, but insurance should cover a new one. Can you guys handle that with the claim?" Suitability gives ROOF_FIRST. The approved fragment offers the partner roofer and a consult. The roofer-referral consult, "we'll come assess the storm damage," and any certified answer about coverage or deductibles now count as solicitation tied to a property-insurance claim.
WHY IT BREAKS: Fla. Stat. 489.147 (SB 76, 2021) bars contractors and their agents from soliciting homeowners to make roof claims, from offering to interpret policies or help with claims, and from waiving deductibles or offering rebates, with fines per violation. Texas Insurance Code 707 bars deductible waivers. The design treats ROOF_FIRST as a safe honest exit. In storm states it is a regulated insurance solicitation path that no profile row governs. Source: flsenate.gov Statutes 489.147.

## A7
TOPIC: PACE (property-tax-assessment) financing: a tax lien, not a loan, with its own solicitation bans, recorded confirmation call and ability-to-pay rules
SCENARIO: The caller says, "My neighbor paid through his property taxes, no credit check. Can I do that? And it's tax-deductible, right?" The lender menu includes a PACE administrator. Needle labels this financing_question. The certified fragments for "pay through taxes" come from the generic loan branch. Any yes/no fragment on deductibility, or "it's a government program," is spoken. The read-back gives a monthly payment, not the annual assessment added to the tax bill ahead of the mortgage.
WHY IT BREAKS: California PACE law (SB 242, AB 1284; Streets & Highways Code 5913 onward, DFPI oversight) bars solicitors from claiming tax deductibility or government sponsorship. It also requires a recorded oral confirmation of key terms and an ability-to-pay determination. The CFPB's 2024 PACE rule brings PACE under TILA ability-to-repay. The Reg Z trigger-term lint assumes a lender loan, not a senior tax lien.

## A8
TOPIC: Homes on tribal trust land: leasehold title, BIA approval for encumbrances, and tribal (not state) consumer, contractor and utility law
SCENARIO: A Navajo Nation homeowner near Shiprock answers. The address resolves to New Mexico, and the most-restrictive state profile loads. "Do you own your home?" "Yes, my family's homesite." The read-back covers a lien/UCC filing with New Mexico. The utility is NTUA, not a state-regulated IOU, so the savings row is wrong. The installer holds a state licence. The e-sign goes out and SALE_PENDING follows.
WHY IT BREAKS: Homesites on trust land are usually leaseholds. Encumbering them needs BIA approval under 25 CFR Part 162, so a state UCC fixture filing may be unenforceable. Tribal sovereignty means tribal law and courts govern consumer, contractor and lien rules, which no state profile captures. The jurisdiction model treats geography as states and counties, with no tribal row at all.

## A9
TOPIC: Texas homestead constitutional lien rules: SMS e-sign cannot meet the required signing location, waiting period, spousal consent and rescission steps
SCENARIO: A Houston couple's homestead. One spouse affirms the read-back for a financed rooftop system with a lien. CONTRACT_SENT sends SMS OTP envelopes, and both sign on their phones the same evening. Install waits out the federal 3-day window. The lien is filed. Nothing in the graph knows that Texas treats a lien on a homestead improvement as a constitutional matter.
WHY IT BREAKS: Texas Constitution art. XVI §50(a)(5) and Property Code 53.254 to 53.255 require, for a lien on existing homestead improvements: a written contract signed before work starts; consent of both spouses given "in the manner of a conveyance"; a 5-day wait after application; signing at a lender's, attorney's or title company's office; and a 3-day rescission. A same-night phone e-sign voids the lien. Source: statutes.capitol.texas.gov CN.16.50; 7 TAC 152.15.

## A10
TOPIC: Travelling or roaming callers: calling-hours rules apply at the called party's physical location, which neither the address nor the area code gives
SCENARIO: A Phoenix snowbird with a 602 number is in Lisbon. The pre-dial gate checks Arizona and the area code: 2 p.m., cleared. In Lisbon it is 9 p.m. or later; on a second campaign wave it is 2 a.m. The groggy caller says "I'm in Portugal, it's the middle of the night." Needle labels small_talk. Later the e-sign arrives while the caller is abroad, the cancellation window runs out unnoticed, and the empty house gets an install.
WHY IT BREAKS: 47 CFR 64.1200(c)(1) and TSR 310.4(c) measure calling hours by local time at the called party's location, not their billing address. A roaming mobile's location is unknowable before dialing, and when the caller says it out loud no event changes anything. The "most restrictive candidate jurisdiction" set only covers address and number geography, so the per-call compliance invariant fails for travellers, military deployments and snowbirds.
